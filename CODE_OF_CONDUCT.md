# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include demonstrating empathy and kindness, respecting differing opinions and experiences, giving and accepting constructive feedback, accepting responsibility and apologizing for mistakes, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or imagery, trolling or insulting comments, harassment, publishing another person's private information without permission, and other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit, or reject contributions and interactions that are inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful. Maintainers will communicate moderation reasons when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces and when an individual officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report abuse privately to the project owner through the contact method shown on [@vicemora97's GitHub profile](https://github.com/vicemora97). All complaints will be reviewed promptly and fairly. Maintainers must respect the reporter's privacy and security.

Enforcement may range from a private correction or warning to a temporary or permanent ban, depending on the impact and pattern of behavior.
