# Flac Cast trademark policy

Flac Cast source code is licensed under `GPL-3.0-or-later`. That license permits copying, modification, and redistribution of the code, but it does not grant permission to use the project's name or visual identity in a way that suggests an unofficial build, fork, service, or organization is produced, sponsored, or endorsed by the Flac Cast project.

The name **Flac Cast**, the official application icon, and the project's distinctive release branding are referred to here as the **Project Marks**. This policy applies to the extent those marks are protected by applicable trademark, passing-off, or unfair-competition law. It does not claim that any mark is registered in every jurisdiction.

Under section 7 of GPLv3, no trademark license is granted beyond the descriptive use necessary to identify Flac Cast and comply with attribution requirements.

## Uses that do not require permission

You may:

- truthfully refer to Flac Cast in articles, reviews, documentation, compatibility statements, and technical discussion;
- state that another project is based on, derived from, or compatible with Flac Cast;
- link to the official repository or display unaltered screenshots for commentary or instruction;
- redistribute an unmodified official release while preserving its license, notices, checksums, and source link, provided you do not imply that you are the official distributor or maintainer; and
- use the name in a fork's historical attribution, for example, “Based on Flac Cast.”

## Forks and modified builds

A public fork or modified binary must use a distinct primary product name and icon. It must also state prominently that it is unofficial and is not endorsed by the Flac Cast project. Package identifiers, installer metadata, download pages, and application-store listings should be changed wherever retaining them could confuse users about the publisher.

The Project Marks must not be used:

- as the primary name or logo of a modified application or competing service;
- to publish a modified binary that appears to be an official Flac Cast release;
- in a domain name, social-media account, repository, or store listing likely to cause confusion about project ownership; or
- to claim sponsorship, certification, partnership, or endorsement that has not been granted in writing.

## No endorsement

Mention of Google Cast, Chromecast, Electron, FFmpeg, LRCLIB, or another third-party project does not imply that those parties endorse Flac Cast. Their names and marks belong to their respective owners.

## Questions and enforcement

Good-faith community use is welcome. If you are unsure whether a proposed use is acceptable, contact the repository owner through the official GitHub profile before publishing it. Reports of impersonation or confusing distribution should use GitHub's private vulnerability-reporting channel when confidentiality is needed.

The project may revise this policy as its branding and community evolve. Changes do not alter the GPL license that applies to source-code versions already distributed.
